Audit & Traceability¶

This area restores the reconstruction layer carried by the historical 68-page Technical Reference without forcing the main methodology back into an audit-manual layout.

Authority boundary. These pages are non-normative human-readable views. In any discrepancy, the certified Architecture, Manual, Project Evidence Base, exact certified commits/trees/packages, and run-specific Gate authorities control.

Reader pathways¶

  • Scientists: use the main Methodology, Figures 1–2, then Source & reference register and model/assurance sections.
  • Senior developers: use Architecture ↔ Manual crosswalk, Manual requirements, Playbooks & checklists, and Certified identities.
  • Auditors: use the conservation crosswalk, AR/DM registries, source/evidence register, certified identities, and historical QA receipt.
  • NCS reviewers/editors: use the main methodological contribution/reproducibility sections, Figures 1–2, Source register, limitations, and conservation/readback evidence.

Public evidence and disclosure boundary

This public documentation projection retains the 15 methodology and audit pages and the frozen, current figure assets. The scientific and certified source authorities remain unchanged.

Approved figure-generator code and selected machine-readable records are distributed only within the versioned reproducibility supplement. Other private JSON inventories, raw QA and independent-review receipts, auxiliary text files, and the five internally rendered evidence pages remain excluded from this public release. The older Figure 1 v1.0.2 assets are superseded by Figure 1 v1.0.3.

The names of controlled research records remain visible where needed for traceability, but those names are not downloadable links. Retained records are accessible only through the project’s applicable authorization process; public presentation does not grant scientific execution or release authority.
