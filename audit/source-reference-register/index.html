Source-governance rules¶

  • Living guidance must carry access/snapshot identity, freshness review, and prospective impact analysis before it changes an MM rule.
  • DQV is preserved as a W3C Working Group Note, not a Recommendation.
  • The ISO/IEC/IEEE 16326 successor URL is corrected to the official ISO record 75276.
  • FAIR4RS authorship is corrected to Barker, M. as lead author.
  • W3C Data Activity is contextual and does not replace DCAT 3, PROV-O, SHACL, or DQV.
  • ISO standards are used at public-metadata level unless licensed normative text is separately admitted.
  • Citation/alignment never implies compliance, endorsement, certification, or affiliation.
